PAPER AND INK / NEXT CHAPTER

5. PRODUCT IDENTITY
============================================================

Her Keys is an AI-powered Life OS / household chief of staff for women
rebuilding life after separation or divorce.

It is NOT:

- a generic task manager
- a wellness app
- a therapy app
- a generic chatbot
- a family calendar with AI attached
- a budgeting app
- a co-parenting utility
- a collection of dashboards
- a cute planner

Those may be ingredients.

The product is the intelligence connecting and coordinating them.

Core product truth:

HER KEYS HELPS HER STOP HOLDING HER ENTIRE LIFE IN HER HEAD.

Design doctrine:

SOFT ENVIRONMENT.
PRECISE INFORMATION.

SOFT IN APPEARANCE.
PRECISE IN BEHAVIOR.

The woman should feel:

- calm
- capable
- oriented
- supported
- respected
- less mentally loaded

She should NOT feel:

- managed
- judged
- infantilized
- overwhelmed
- trapped in configuration
- like she is using enterprise software

============================================================
6. APPLICATION-WIDE DESIGN PRINCIPLES
============================================================

ONE THING AT A TIME

Every screen should clearly answer:

- What matters here?
- What should I look at first?
- What can I do next?

HIERARCHY BEFORE DECORATION

Important information wins.

CALM DOES NOT MEAN VAGUE

Deadlines, risk, conflicts, consequences and approvals must remain explicit.

REDUCE DECISIONS

Use safe defaults where product semantics permit.

Do not force configuration for ordinary actions.

EXPLAIN INTELLIGENCE

Her Keys should be able to show:

- what it noticed
- what it understood
- what it inferred
- what it recommends
- why
- what needs confirmation
- what requires approval
- what happened

without exposing chain-of-thought.

HUMAN CONTROL

Correction, rejection, approval and adjustment remain obvious.

GRACEFUL COMPLEXITY

Simple situations look simple.

Complexity appears only when the situation genuinely requires it.

============================================================
8. ACCESSIBILITY / CONTRAST FLOOR
============================================================

WCAG AA is the minimum requirement.

At minimum:

- normal/body text: 4.5:1
- large text: 3:1
- UI components and meaningful graphical boundaries: 3:1 where applicable

Do not describe contrast as “looks readable.”

Measure it.

Create a contrast matrix covering every canonical text token against every
surface on which that token is intended to appear.

Report:

PASS / FAIL
ratio
pairing

Attack first:

- muted text on ivory
- secondary text on secondary surfaces
- clay accent text
- any pastel accent
- disabled states
- AI semantic treatments

Meaning must never depend on color alone.

Also ensure:

- practical touch targets
- accessible labels
- roles/states
- dynamic text resilience
- logical reading order
- keyboard avoidance
- long-content behavior
- focus visibility where applicable

============================================================
13. AI SEMANTICS ARE RENDERED, NOT INVENTED
============================================================

The front end is NOT a second semantics authority.

Before building any AI/intelligence presentation component, map it to the
existing frozen Build 4 semantics.

Relevant stored provenance producer vocabulary includes:

- onboarding
- user-action
- talk-it-out
- system-derived
- import-sync
- ai-inference
- demo-seed
- automation
- legacy-unknown

Relevant confidence vocabulary is:

- possible
- likely
- established

Do NOT introduce presentation-layer confidence tiers.

Do NOT invent percentages.

User-stated facts do not acquire fake confidence simply because a UI would
like to display one.

The presentation system must render the durable semantics faithfully.

REQUIRED TRACEABILITY TABLE

For every intelligence presentation pattern, record:

- UI state/pattern
- exact durable/domain source
- producer/confidence/action type it reads
- display treatment
- correction/approval affordance
- whether it is FACT, INFERENCE, RECOMMENDATION, DECISION, EXECUTION or OUTCOME

Rule:

NO DURABLE/DOMAIN SEMANTIC COUNTERPART = DO NOT INVENT ONE IN THE UI.
