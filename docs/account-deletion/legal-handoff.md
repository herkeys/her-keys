The Apple and Google official guidance reviewed did not specify a numerical requirement authorizing an autonomous change to the 30-day recovery policy. Apple accepts a reasonable disclosed processing period with confirmation. This does not establish that a mandatory 30-day grace plus processing satisfies every applicable privacy-law deadline. Legal must assess jurisdictions and any statutory without-undue-delay/response obligations before launch; if a conflict is confirmed, obtain approval for a separately designed immediate-erasure or explicitly chosen recovery option rather than silently shortening or extending the contract.
